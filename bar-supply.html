Bar Supply

A great bar starts long before the first drink is poured. We stock, source, and deliver everything a serious cocktail program needs — down to the syrup ratios and the shape of the ice — so your bar runs like one that's been open for years, even if it opened an hour ago.

Our syrup program alone runs dozens deep: classic simple and demerara, brown butter and toasted pecan, strawberry-basil and charred pineapple, seasonal one-offs built around whatever's actually in season. Pair them with a rotating bitters selection — from workhorse aromatic and orange to small-batch mole and smoked chili — and even a simple two-ingredient drink starts to taste considered.

For anything that needs more than a shake, we build purées and reductions to spec — hibiscus, blackberry-sage, roasted stone fruit — along with infusion kits for fat-washed spirits, botanical steeps, and barrel-aged builds you can run entirely in-house.

Want a program with a little theater? We supply the foams, tinctures, and modernist tools — think egg-white alternatives, aquafaba rigs, and clarified-milk kits — for bars that want texture and spectacle without a science degree.

None of it matters without the right ice. We run a premium ice program — large-format clear cubes, crushed pebble ice, blocks cut to order — plus the glassware, jiggers, shakers, strainers, and bar tools to back it all up.
