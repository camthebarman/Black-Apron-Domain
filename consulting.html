Consulting

Not every bar problem gets fixed with a better cocktail. Sometimes it's the software, sometimes it's the schedule, sometimes it's a menu quietly losing you money every shift. We consult on the full operation — not just what's in the glass.

Whether you're opening a new program, fixing a bar that's bleeding margin, or just want a second set of eyes on how things run, we work alongside your team instead of handing you a binder and disappearing.

Where We Dig In

POS systems that actually fit how your bar runs, inventory and cost-tracking software that catches shrinkage before it costs you a shift's profit, scheduling tools that stop the Sunday-night group text. We help you pick the stack, set it up, and train your team to actually use it.

Pour cost, labor cost, prep lists, opening and closing checklists, vendor relationships — the stuff that decides whether a bar is profitable or just busy. We build the SOPs, tighten the workflows, and train your staff so the bar runs the same whether you're behind it or not.

Menu development, pricing strategy, and positioning that gets your bar noticed for the right reasons. Whether you're launching a new program, relaunching an old one, or just trying to grow margins without raising prices across the board, we build the plan.

How We Work

Audit & Assessment

A full look at your current operation — menu, costs, systems, and staff — before we recommend a single change.

Custom Roadmap

A prioritized plan built around your bar, your budget, and your timeline, not a generic template.

Hands-On Training

We train your team directly, so the changes stick after we're gone.

Ongoing Support

Check-ins and adjustments as the program runs, not a one-and-done report.
