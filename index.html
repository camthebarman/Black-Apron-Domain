Who We Are

The apron comes off, the craft doesn't.

Black Apron Bar Co. was built on a simple idea: a great drink shouldn't require a lecture. We bring real bartending craft — balanced builds, proper technique, honest ingredients — to bars, backyards, and everything in between, without the stiff-collar attitude that usually comes with it.

Every pour is deliberate. Every event is run like the details matter, because they do. Whether we're stocking your home bar or running service for two hundred guests, the standard doesn't move.

— The Black Apron Bar Co. team
