Private Bartending

Great hosting means never leaving your own party to make drinks. Our private bartenders step in as front-of-house staff for your event — professional, personable, and fully capable of running a bar on their own so you don't have to.

Every bartender we send is trained, insured, and comfortable working a room, not just a shaker. They handle guest interaction, pacing, and cleanup with the same care they put into the pour — because the best bar service is the kind guests barely notice is being managed.

Menus are built around your event, not a template — classic builds, a signature cocktail named after the guest of honor, a fully dry option for anyone who wants one. We scale staffing to guest count, so a dinner party for twelve gets the same attention as a backyard wedding for a hundred and fifty.

We've worked dinner parties, milestone birthdays, engagement parties, holiday gatherings, and corporate happy hours — anywhere a host wants a real bar experience without staffing it themselves.
