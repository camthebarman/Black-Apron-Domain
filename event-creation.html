Event Creation

Not every event needs a bar — some need an experience built around one. We design and run full-scale beverage programming and hosted experiences, from an intimate tasting to a 300-plus guest production.

Guided tastings are where most of our clients start — structured wine flights, spirit tastings built around a theme (mezcal, aged rum, single-barrel bourbon), or beer tastings that walk guests through style and technique, led by someone who actually knows the category.

For groups that want to get hands-on, we run cocktail-making classes and full bartending courses — team-building events, private lessons, or staff training for bars and restaurants building out their own program.

We also produce themed nights end to end: murder mystery dinners with a full script and cast, trivia nights built around your group's interests, and other hosted formats that turn "we should do something fun" into an actual night out.

And for the big ones — weddings, galas, corporate functions, festivals — we run full-scale events for 300-plus guests: multiple bar stations, a staffed team, and a beverage program designed to move fast without losing quality.
