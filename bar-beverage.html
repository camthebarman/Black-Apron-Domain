Bar & Beverage

We run the bar — literally. From an intimate dinner party to a 300-guest wedding, our team shows up, sets up, and runs service front to back, so you're a guest at your own event instead of the one stuck behind the counter.

Every bartender we send is trained, insured, and built for the room — comfortable managing a line, reading the crowd, and keeping pace without losing the craft. Menus are built around your event: classic builds, a signature drink named after the happy couple, dry options for anyone who wants one.

We staff to the size of the room — a dinner party for twelve gets the same attention as a wedding for three hundred. Multiple stations, a full team, and a program designed to move fast without cutting corners.

Three Ways We Pour

Not everyone's drinking, and that shouldn't mean a sad lemonade station. Our mocktail program gets the same bar treatment as the cocktail menu — fresh juices, house syrups, proper garnish, and builds designed to actually taste like something, not an afterthought. Great for dry weddings, daytime events, or just giving every guest a real option.

The core of what we do. A full bar built around your event — classic builds, a seasonal menu, a signature cocktail or two, all run by bartenders who know their way around a shaker and a room full of people. Scales from a backyard dinner party to a full wedding reception.

For the end of the night, the morning after, or a daytime event that needs something other than alcohol, we run a proper coffee and espresso station — drip, pour-over, espresso drinks, and a cold option, staffed with the same care as the bar.
